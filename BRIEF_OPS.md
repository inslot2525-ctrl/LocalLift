## Scripts (edit before using; never claim anything untrue)

**Shop pitch (30 seconds, say it with the phone in hand):**
"Diwali offers need posters and WhatsApp messages. Tell me your shop name and your offer and I will make the whole pack in your language right now. You see a preview free. If you like it, it is Rs [price] and you get all sizes ready to post."

**Rep recruitment message:**
"We are building LocalLift for Diwali: ready-to-post offer posters and messages for local shops. If you bring shops and set up their packs, you earn [30]% of every confirmed sale, paid by UPI after the payment is confirmed. No investment. Want to try it this week? I will send you your link and a one-page script."

**Objection handling (three common ones):**
- "I already use a free poster app." -> "Do you have time to make six sizes in two languages? We do it in minutes and you only pay if you like the preview."
- "Too expensive." -> Ask what they would pay, record the number, offer the starter pack.
- "Later." -> Ask for a date; Dhanteras is Nov 6, so the posters are needed before then.

**Commission terms (simple, written, shared with each rep; not legal advice):** rep earns [30]% of each confirmed sale made with their code; payout by UPI after the customer's payment is confirmed; no payout for self-referrals; terms can change with notice. We have not researched tax or legal requirements for commissions; check before scaling.
